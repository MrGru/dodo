# Story E9.4

**Canonical ID:** `e9.4`
**Status:** open-question (not an engineering task)
**Epic:** E9 — Build, Release & Licensing engineering

## Story

**As a** maintainer preparing to distribute a built binary **I want** the GPL-3.0-or-later
question actually resolved **so that** distribution doesn't happen on an unexamined assumption
either way.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC-1**: Someone with the authority to make a licensing decision reviews the verified chain
      `gpui -> sum_tree -> ztracing -> zlog` (GPL-3.0-or-later) against dodo's own MIT source and
      states a distribution posture.
- [ ] **AC-2**: `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md` is updated to record the decision (not just the question).
- [ ] **AC-3**: If the decision requires it, `deny.toml` gains an explicit `allow`/`exceptions`
      entry — until then, it deliberately has none, so `cargo deny` keeps surfacing the chain.

## Where it lives (today, as an open question)

- `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, `deny.toml`, `LICENSE`

## Notes

- This is not a coding story — no engineering change resolves it. `Cargo.lock`-only pinning
  (ADR-001) and the `rusqlite`/`sqlx` conflict (ADR-008) are related dependency-graph constraints
  worth knowing before touching anything nearby, but neither resolves this question either.
